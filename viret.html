Tribute to Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is one of the most successful cricketers in modern history. Born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India, he is known for his aggressive batting style and incredible consistency. Kohli has led the Indian cricket team to numerous victories and is celebrated for his remarkable records in all formats of the game.

Achievements:
- Fastest century in ODIs
- Most runs in a calendar year in ODIs
- Leading run-scorer in IPL history
- ICC ODI Player of the Year multiple times
